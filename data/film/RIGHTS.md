# Film data and rights

OmaRAW includes independently developed film simulations based on numerical
film-response information in published manufacturer technical sheets. The
simulations use OmaRAW's own rendering model, calibration and display mapping.
They are interpretations of film behaviour, with documented approximations.

## Sources and attribution

The [film source index](NOTICE.md) identifies the manufacturers and publications.
Each stock's `SOURCE.md` records the edition, measured quantities, extraction
method and any spectral substitutions. [Digital-model provenance](DIGITAL-SOURCE.md)
describes the digital cinema variants.

The source data cover Eastman Kodak, Kodak Alaris, Fujifilm and Agfa publications.
OmaRAW digitises numerical curves, calibrates their axes and calculates its own
tables. The manufacturers' PDFs, page artwork, logos and third-party comparison
LUTs are not distributed. Plotted PNGs in this tree are generated by OmaRAW from
the extracted values. The underlying film measurements were not made by OmaRAW.

## Scope of rights

OmaRAW uses the numerical film-response information as input to its own
simulation. It does not claim ownership of the manufacturers' publications,
trademarks or other protected material. Copyright in a publication and rights
in the numerical information it describes are distinct questions; public
availability alone is not an open-data licence.

No express manufacturer redistribution licence, affiliation or endorsement is
claimed. Manufacturer and stock names identify the sources and belong to their
respective owners. OmaRAW's code licence does not relicense third-party source
material or trademarks.

The generation tools also use separately licensed colour-science resources.
Their licences and credits are recorded in [COLOUR-SOURCES.md](COLOUR-SOURCES.md)
and [COLOUR-SCIENCE-LICENSE.txt](COLOUR-SCIENCE-LICENSE.txt). Preserve the applicable
licences and source attribution when redistributing those resources.
