# Agfa Print CP30 colour print film

- Agfa Motion Picture Division, Technical Data CP30, 18/10/2003 (cp30_en.pdf).
- Curves extracted as vector paths with `tools/film/extract-curves.py`; the
  sensitometric axes from the tick labels (comma decimals, density × 100),
  the spectral axes from the rendered gridlines.
- `sensitometric-*.csv`: Status A density vs relative log exposure.
- `sensitivity-*.csv`: log sensitivity vs wavelength.
- `dyes-*.csv`: diffuse spectral density, typical densities for a neutral
  subject at D 3.00 (not peak-normalised), plus the neutral itself.
- LAD aim (Status A, xenon): 1.15 red, 1.05 green, 1.05 blue; balanced for
  3200 K tungsten printing light.
