# Kodak Vision Color Print Film 2383 / 3383

- Publication H-1-2383t, March 2005 (Eastman Kodak Company), archived copy from
  the US National Archives preservation resources (2383-TI.pdf). The 2022
  revision (H-1-2383) shows the same curves as raster images.
- Curves extracted as vector paths from the PDF with `tools/film/extract-curves.py`;
  axes calibrated from the tick labels.
- `sensitometric-*.csv`: Status A density vs log exposure (lux-seconds), exposure
  1/500 s tungsten plus heat-absorbing glass No. 2043 and Series 1700 filter, Process ECP-2D.
- `sensitivity-*.csv`: log sensitivity vs wavelength, tungsten 1/50 s, ECP-2D, density 1.0.
- `dyes-*.csv`: diffuse spectral density vs wavelength; dyes normalised to form a
  visual neutral of 1.0 for a xenon-arc viewing illuminant; C, M, Y peak-normalised.
- LAD aim (Status A) on this stock: 1.09 red, 1.06 green, 1.03 blue (Kodak H-61B).
