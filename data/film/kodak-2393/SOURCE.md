# Kodak Vision Premier Color Print Film 2393

- Publication H-1-2393t, September 1998 (Eastman Kodak Company).
- Curves extracted as vector paths with `tools/film/extract-curves.py`; sensitometric
  and dye axes from tick labels, sensitivity axes from the rendered frame.
- `sensitometric-*.csv`: Status A density vs log exposure (lux-seconds), 1/500 s
  tungsten plus heat-absorbing glass No. 2043 and Series 1700 filter, Process ECP-2B.
- `sensitivity-*.csv`: log sensitivity vs wavelength, tungsten 1/50 s, ECP-2B, density 1.0.
- `dyes-*.csv`: diffuse spectral density, typical densities for a midscale neutral
  subject and D-min, ECP-2B.
- LAD aim (Status A): 1.09 red, 1.06 green, 1.03 blue.
