# Auto exposure

**Auto** in Develop estimates a starting exposure from the photograph's light
values while protecting bright areas. It sets a new exposure rather than adding
the same correction every time you press it.

Import already applies the camera's starting correction. You do not need to
press Auto if the imported image looks right. Auto is a whole-frame estimate:
night scenes, snow and small subjects can need a different result. Adjust
Exposure afterwards to suit the photograph.

White balance and camera calibration affect the estimate. Creative film looks,
soft proof and monitor settings do not determine its brightness target. The
rest of your edit stays in place, and Undo restores the previous exposure.
